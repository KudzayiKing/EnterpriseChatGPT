# Serivisi z'Abanyamahanga za Irembo

## Gusaba Viza
**Ibisobanuro:** Abanyamahanga bashobora gusaba ubwoko butandukanye bwa viza bwo kwinjira mu Rwanda (Ubukerarugendo, Ubucuruzi, Inama, n'ibindi).
**Ibikenewe:**
- Pasiporo ifite agaciro (byibuze amezi 6)
- Ifoto y'irangamuntu igezweho
- Ibaruwa y'ubutumire (ku bucuruzi/inama)
- Icyemeza cy'aho uzaba
**Igihe cyo gutegereza:** Iminsi 3-7 y'akazi
**Igiciro:** Bitandukanye bitewe n'ubwoko bwa viza ($50 - $100)

## Gusaba Pasiporo y'u Rwanda
**Ibisobanuro:** Gusaba pasiporo nshya cyangwa kuyishyira mu bihe by'abanyarwanda.
**Ibikenewe:**
- Irangamuntu ry'igihugu
- Icyemeza cy'kwishyura
- Ibaruwa y'icyifuzo (ku bana bato)
**Igihe cyo gutegereza:** Iminsi 2-4 y'akazi (mu Rwanda)
**Igiciro:** Amafaranga 75,000 y'u Rwanda (Isanzwe imyaka 5), Amafaranga 100,000 y'u Rwanda (Isanzwe imyaka 10)

## Irangamuntu ry'Abanyamahanga (Foreigner ID)
**Ibisobanuro:** Abanyamahanga baba mu Rwanda igihe kirenze amezi 6 bagomba gusaba Irangamuntu ry'Abanyamahanga.
**Ibikenewe:**
- Amakuru ya Viza ifite agaciro
- Ibaruwa y'umukoresha cyangwa icyemeza cy'ubucuruzi
- Kopi ya Pasiporo
**Igihe cyo gutegereza:** Iminsi 14 y'akazi
**Igiciro:** Amafaranga 50,000 y'u Rwanda

## Laissez-Passer
**Ibisobanuro:** Icyangombwa cy'urugendo ku banyarwanda bagenda mu bihugu bituranye (EAC).
**Ibikenewe:**
- Irangamuntu ry'igihugu
- Ifoto y'irangamuntu
**Igihe cyo gutegereza:** Umunsi 1 w'akazi (Itangwa ako kanya mu bigo bimwe)
**Igiciro:** Amafaranga 10,000 y'u Rwanda

## Ubwenegihugu bw'u Rwanda ku Bwunganizi
**Ibisobanuro:** Abanyamahanga bashobora gusaba ubwenegihugu bw'u Rwanda hashingiwe ku bukwe, ubwana bwemera, cyangwa gutura igihe kirekire.
**Ibikenewe:**
- Icyangombwa cy'ivuka
- Icyangombwa cy'abapolisi cyo mu gihugu ukomoka
- Icyemeza cy'aho utuye mu Rwanda (imyaka 5+)
- Icyangombwa cy'ubukwe (niba bikenewe)
**Igihe cyo gutegereza:** Amezi 3-6
**Igiciro:** Amafaranga yo gusaba arakurikizwa

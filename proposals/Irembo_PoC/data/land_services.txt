# Irembo Land Services

## Land Registration
**Description:** This service allows citizens to register their land for the first time. It is mandatory for all land parcels in Rwanda to be registered.
**Requirements:**
- Proof of identity (ID card or Passport)
- Supporting documents proving ownership (e.g., cell/sector letter, sale agreement)
- Witnesses statements if applicable
**Processing Time:** 7-14 working days
**Price:** 5,000 RWF

## Title Transfer - Voluntary Sale
**Description:** Used when a land title needs to be transferred from one owner to another due to a voluntary sale.
**Requirements:**
- Valid land title of the seller
- Sale agreement signed by both parties and notarized
- ID copies of both seller and buyer
- Proof of payment of transfer fees
**Processing Time:** 1-5 working days
**Price:** 30,000 RWF (Transfer fee) + Notary fees

## Title Transfer - Subscription/Donation
**Description:** Transfer of land title as a gift or donation.
**Requirements:**
- Donation agreement/letter
- ID copies of donor and recipient
- Original land title
**Processing Time:** 5 working days
**Price:** 5,000 RWF

## Land Subdivision
**Description:** Service to split one parcel of land into two or more smaller parcels.
**Requirements:**
- Original land title
- Approved subdivision plan by a surveyor
- Payment of subdivision fees
**Processing Time:** 10 working days
**Price:** 10,000 RWF per resulting parcel

## Rectification of Boundaries
**Description:** Used to correct the boundaries of a land parcel if they were incorrectly recorded.
**Requirements:**
- Application letter explaining the error
- Report from a surveyor
- Consent from neighbors if boundaries affect them
**Processing Time:** 14 working days

## Merging of Land Parcels
**Description:** Combining two or more adjacent parcels owned by the same person into one title.
**Requirements:**
- Original titles of all parcels
- Approved merging plan
**Processing Time:** 7 working days

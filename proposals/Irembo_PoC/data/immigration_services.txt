# Irembo Immigration Services

## Visa Application
**Description:** Foreign nationals can apply for various types of visas to enter Rwanda (Tourism, Business, Conference, etc.).
**Requirements:**
- Valid passport (at least 6 months validity)
- Recent passport-size photo
- Invitation letter (for business/conference)
- Proof of accommodation
**Processing Time:** 3-7 working days
**Price:** Varies by visa type ($50 - $100)

## Rwandan Passport Application
**Description:** Application for new passport or renewal for Rwandan citizens.
**Requirements:**
- National ID card
- Proof of payment
- Recommendation letter (for minors)
**Processing Time:** 2-4 working days (within Rwanda)
**Price:** 75,000 RWF (Ordinary 5 years), 100,000 RWF (Ordinary 10 years)

## Resident ID Card (Foreigner ID)
**Description:** Foreigners residing in Rwanda for more than 6 months must apply for a Resident ID.
**Requirements:**
- Valid Visa details
- Employer letter or proof of business
- Passport copy
**Processing Time:** 14 working days
**Price:** 50,000 RWF

## Laissez-Passer
**Description:** Travel document for Rwandans traveling to neighboring countries (EAC).
**Requirements:**
- National ID
- Passport photo
**Processing Time:** 1 working day (Instant issuance at some centers)
**Price:** 10,000 RWF

## Rwandan Nationality by Acquisition
**Description:** Foreigners can apply for Rwandan nationality based on marriage, adoption, or long-term residence.
**Requirements:**
- Birth certificate
- Police clearance from country of origin
- Proof of residence in Rwanda (5+ years)
- Marriage certificate (if applicable)
**Processing Time:** 3-6 months
**Price:** Application fee applies
